# Finding priorities

**Trigger:** Load only when priority is ambiguous, findings must be compared, severity levels are disputed, or priority materially affects the user's decision.

Use only:

- `Blocker` — prevents safe execution, auditing, verification, or completion;
- `Critical` — severe confirmed or strongly supported harm;
- `Major` — significant impact without blocking current work;
- `Minor` — limited but actionable material impact.

Priority reflects material impact and likelihood; category alone does not determine it. Preserve uncertainty in evidence and confidence rather than hiding it in priority.
