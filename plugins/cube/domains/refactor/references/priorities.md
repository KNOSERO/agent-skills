# Proposal priorities

Load when severity or priority is ambiguous, materially affects ordering, or needs explicit explanation.

Use only a level that clarifies actual impact:

- `Blocker` — the issue prevents safely executing or verifying the planned change;
- `Critical` — correctness or change safety has exceptionally high impact;
- `Major` — material maintainability, responsibility, boundary, dependency, or development-friction impact;
- `Minor` — a useful but limited improvement.

Do not raise priority because of stylistic disagreement. Do not force priority distinctions when they add no decision value.
