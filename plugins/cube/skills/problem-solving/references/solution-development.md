# Solution development

Load this reference when more than one materially plausible solution exists or the preferred approach requires meaningful trade-off analysis.

Generate only candidates that could satisfy the goal and confirmed constraints. Reject candidates that fail a decisive requirement. Compare the remaining candidates on material differences such as correctness, business fit, compatibility, scope, complexity, risk, operational impact, maintainability, architectural fit, and cost. Do not compare incidental differences or manufacture alternatives when one option is clearly superior.

Recommend the option best supported by evidence, confirmed requirements and decisions, process context, dependencies, and trade-offs. State meaningful consequences, assumptions, and what must be verified before execution. A recommendation does not replace an unresolved user decision.
