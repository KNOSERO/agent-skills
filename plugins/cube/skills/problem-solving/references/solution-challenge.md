# Solution challenge

Load this reference when the proposed solution could materially fail because of contracts, dependencies, edge conditions, process constraints, failure modes, high-impact assumptions, compatibility, or architectural impact.

Check the preferred solution against the original goal, confirmed constraints, affected process, existing contracts, important dependencies, material failure paths, and relevant edge conditions. Look for evidence that would reject or materially change it. Correct or reject the proposal when the check exposes a failure; otherwise record the remaining material risks and validation needs. Keep the challenge proportional to the risk.
